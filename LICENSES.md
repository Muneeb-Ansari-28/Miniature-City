## Optional CC0 3D Asset Packs (Drop-in Compatible)
This project runs with zero external models by default. When upgrading via `public/models/` and `src/config/assetManifest.ts`, the following CC0 (Public Domain) packs are recommended:

| Category | Recommended Pack | License |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Buildings & Roads | Kenney *City Kit (Commercial / Suburban / Roads)* or KayKit *City Builder Bits* | CC0 1.0 |
| Restaurants & Patios | KayKit *Restaurant Bits* & Kenney *Food Kit* | CC0 1.0 |
| Vehicles | Kenney *Car Kit* or Quaternius *Ultimate Stylized Cars* | CC0 1.0 |
| Citizens | Kenney *Blocky Characters* or Quaternius *Universal Animation Library* | CC0 1.0 |
| Ambient Audio | Procedural Web Audio synthesizer (built-in) or Freesound CC0 ambience | CC0 1.0 |
